What do we do?

Standup Sessions — Monday 6-7:30pm

Every Monday evening, we meet in to discuss all things stand-up related!! We watch famous (and student) comedy sets, play some warm-up games, and there's even a chance to perform new material, with no pressure at all! The material we write goes into our regular stand-up shows on campus, as well as off-campus shows, including the Edinburgh Fringe!!

Sketch Sessions — Friday 6-7:30pm

Every Friday evening, all of us cool kids gather in to discuss all things sketch-related!! We write sketches for our termly sketch shows, and watch sketches to learn how to write really funny stuff. Which is why all of our sketches are really funny.
This isn't everyone!! See the exec page for more info about all of us!!
